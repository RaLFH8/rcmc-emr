- Click "Clear data"

Firefox:
- Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- Click "Clear Data"
- Check "Cached Web Content"
- Click "Clear"

Edge:
- Settings → Privacy, search, and services
- Click "Choose what to clear"
- Select "Cached images and files"
- Click "Clear now"
