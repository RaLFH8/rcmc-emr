✅ YES! New patients from online booking WILL appear in:

1. ✅ PATIENTS MODULE
   - Shows all Active patients
   - New patients have status: 'Active'
   - Searchable by name, phone, patient number
   - Has auto-generated patient number (P000001, P000002, etc.)

2. ✅ APPOINTMENTS MODULE (Waiting/Queue)
   - Shows all appointments for selected date
   - Includes patient details (name, contact, etc.)
   - Shows "Pending" badge for online bookings
   - Can be confirmed/rejected by staff

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  HOW IT WORKS                                                        ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

STEP 1: Patient books online
   └─> Fills form with personal details
   └─> Selects doctor, date, time

STEP 2: System creates patient record
   └─> Inserts into 'patients' table
   └─> Sets status: 'Active'
   └─> Generates patient number: P000001

STEP 3: System creates appointment
   └─> Links to patient via patient_id
   └─> Sets booking_status: 'pending'
   └─> Sets status: 'Scheduled'

STEP 4: Patient appears in system
   └─> ✅ Visible in Patients module
   └─> ✅ Visible in Appointments module
   └─> ✅ Searchable and manageable
