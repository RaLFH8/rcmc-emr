THE FIX (Takes 2 minutes):
===========================

STEP 1: Find Your Service Account Email
----------------------------------------
1. Open: rcmc-emr/server/google-credentials.json
2. Look for "client_email"
3. Copy the email (it looks like: something@something.iam.gserviceaccount.com)

STEP 2: Share the Folder
-------------------------
1. Go to Google Drive: https://drive.google.com
2. Find your "Lab Results" folder (or search for folder ID: 1JBKtpUdImoQc1LPzyrmVpOsUCYeTky64)
3. Right-click the folder → Click "Share"
4. Paste the service account email
5. Give it "Editor" permission
6. Click "Send" (ignore the warning about sending to a service account)

STEP 3: Try Uploading Again
----------------------------
Go back to http://localhost:3001 and try uploading a lab result PDF.

IT WILL WORK! 🎉

WHY THIS IS NEEDED:
===================
Service accounts don't have their own Google Drive storage.
They can only access folders that are explicitly shared with them.

Once you share the folder, the service account can upload files to it.

ALTERNATIVE (If you can't find the folder):
============================================
Create a NEW folder and share it:

1. Go to Google Drive
2. Create a new folder called "RCMC Lab Results"
3. Right-click → Share
4. Add the service account email with Editor permission
5. Open the folder
6. Copy the folder ID from the URL (the long string after /folders/)
7. Update rcmc-emr/server/.env with the new folder ID
8. Restart the backend
