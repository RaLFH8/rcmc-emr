HOW IT WORKS:
=================================================================

AUTOMATIC CALCULATION:
- When you select a patient, system checks their age
- If age >= 60, discount is automatically applied
- If PWD checkbox is checked, discount is applied
- Discount recalculates when:
  * Patient is changed
  * PWD checkbox is toggled
  * Items are added or removed

DISCOUNT FORMULA:
- Subtotal = Sum of all items
- Discount Amount = Subtotal × 20%
- Final Total = Subtotal - Discount Amount

EXAMPLE:
- Subtotal: ₱1,000.00
- Discount (Senior Citizen - 20%): -₱200.00
- Total Amount: ₱800.00
