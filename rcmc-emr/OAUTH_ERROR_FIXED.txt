========================================
✅ HOW TO FIX: Error 403 access_denied
========================================

WHAT'S HAPPENING:
=================
Your OAuth app is in "Testing" mode.
You need to add your email as a test user.

This is NORMAL and takes 1 minute to fix!

QUICK FIX:
==========

Double-click: ADD_TEST_USER_NOW.bat

This will:
1. Open Google Cloud Console
2. Show you where to add your email
3. Guide you through the process

OR DO IT MANUALLY:
==================

1. Go to: https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials/consent?project=rcmc-lab-results

2. Scroll to "Test users" section

3. Click "+ ADD USERS"

4. Enter: rcmcrecords@gmail.com

5. Click "SAVE"

6. Done!

AFTER ADDING YOUR EMAIL:
=========================

1. Go back to http://localhost:3001
2. Click "Lab Results"
3. Click "Upload Lab Result"
4. Google will now show the permission popup
5. Click "Allow"
6. Upload works! 🎉

WHY THIS HAPPENS:
=================
Google requires apps in "Testing" mode to have
an approved list of test users. This is a security
feature to prevent unauthorized access during
development.

Once you add your email, you can use the app
without any restrictions!

ALTERNATIVE (OPTIONAL):
=======================
If you want ANYONE to use it without adding them:

1. Go to OAuth consent screen
2. Click "PUBLISH APP"
3. Click "CONFIRM"

Note: You'll see "unverified app" warning, but
it's safe for personal/internal use.

========================================
READY TO FIX!
========================================

Double-click: ADD_TEST_USER_NOW.bat

Or visit: https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials/consent?project=rcmc-lab-results

Add your email and you're done! 🚀
