🧪 TESTING THE SOAP NOTE WORKFLOW:

1. Login to your EMR
2. Go to Appointments page
3. Switch to "Patient Queue" view (toggle at top)
4. Click "Start Consultation" on any waiting patient
   → SOAP Note modal should open ✨

5. Fill in the four sections:
   S - SUBJECTIVE: Patient's complaint
   O - OBJECTIVE: Vital signs (BP, Temp, HR, RR)
   A - ASSESSMENT: Diagnosis
   P - PLAN: Treatment plan

6. Click "Save & Continue"
   → Patient moves to "In Progress" ✨

7. Click "Complete" button
   → Review modal opens ✨

8. Review the consultation summary
9. Click "Complete & Save to Medical History"
   → Patient moves to "Completed" ✨

10. Go to Patients page → View patient details
    → Medical history shows SOAP note with correct doctor ✨
